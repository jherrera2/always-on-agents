# What you can use it for

> Each pattern is one I run, kept generic.

A persistent agent earns its place when the work is **recurring, spread over days, and needs
memory** — not a one-off question. These are the shapes that have worked for me.

| Pattern | What the agent does | Why an always-on agent (not a chat) |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal assistant** | Reads email each morning, flags deadlines and real people, tracks errands and appointments. | Runs at 7 AM without me asking; remembers what's still open. |
| **Work task manager** | Breaks a to-do list into estimated subtasks, checks what fits my real hours, logs interruptions, writes my weekly meeting recap. | Knows my capacity and history; the recap is ready Thursday morning. |
| **Goal accountability** | A 4 PM menu of three time-boxed tasks toward my long-term goal, a nightly "what moved?" log, a Sunday review. | Consistency is the whole point, and it never forgets to ask. |
| **Household** | A shared grocery list and reminders for two people, in two languages, in a group chat. | Several people talk to the same agent; it keeps one list. |
| **Budget** | Categorizes transactions, spots recurring charges, answers "can we afford X?" | Months of context about our money, private on my own machine. |
| **Content pipeline** | Builds a daily video: script, narration, images, subtitles, ready for review. | A daily cadence with state between runs. |
| **Small business with an allowance** | Picks one venture, runs it for 30 days on a fixed monthly budget, reports spend and revenue every morning, decides keep / pivot / kill. | Long-running experiment with a ledger and a hard spending cap. |
| **Learning coach** | Works through a book with me, keeps chapter notes, turns each chapter into action items and checks in on them. | A book takes weeks; it remembers what I've read, decided and already done. |
| **A family member's assistant** | Answers questions, translates, drafts messages and forms, in their language. | Their own bot, their own memory, separate from mine. |
| **Fleet caretaker** | Curates every agent's memory nightly and checks that scheduled jobs actually ran. | Someone has to watch the watchers. |

## When it's the wrong tool

- **One-off questions.** A normal chat is cheaper and simpler.
- **Anything that must never act without you.** Keep publishing, payments and anything sent in
  your name behind an explicit yes. My agents prepare, I click.
- **Work you won't review.** An agent producing output nobody reads is just spending money.
- **Many agents on day one.** Start with one, make it reliable, then add the next.
